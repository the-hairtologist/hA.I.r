## 🎯 Conversion Funnel Strategy

### Stage 1: Awareness (Cold Traffic)
**Channels**: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube
**Content**: Short-form viral content showing WOW moments
**Goal**: Stop the scroll, spark curiosity

### Stage 2: Consideration (Warm Traffic)
**Channels**: Retargeting ads, email nurture
**Content**: Feature deep-dives, case studies, ROI calculators
**Goal**: Build trust, overcome objections

### Stage 3: Decision (Hot Traffic)
**Channels**: Landing page, demo booking
**Content**: Free trial, video walkthroughs, live chat
**Goal**: Remove friction, start trial

### Stage 4: Retention (Customers)
**Channels**: In-app, email, push notifications
**Content**: Feature tips, success stories, community
**Goal**: Activation, feature adoption, referrals
