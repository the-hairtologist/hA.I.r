# hA.I.r Quick Start Guide

**Welcome to hA.I.r!** This guide will get you up and running in 5 minutes.

---

## For Stylists

### 1. Create Your Account (2 minutes)

**Option A: Email Signup**

1. Go to https://your-app-url.lovable.app
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email and password
4. Check your email for confirmation (auto-confirmed in development)
5. Select "I'm a Stylist"

**Option B: Google Signup (Recommended)**

1. Click "Sign up with Google"
2. Select your Google account
3. Confirm permissions
4. Select "I'm a Stylist"

### 2. Complete Your Profile (1 minute)

1. Add your business name
2. Upload a profile photo
3. Add your location
4. Set your working hours
5. Add specialties (color, cuts, extensions, etc.)
6. Click "Save Profile"

### 3. Add Services & Pricing (1 minute)

1. Go to "Services" page
2. Click "Add Service"
3. Enter service details:
   - Service name (e.g., "Full Color")
   - Duration (e.g., 120 minutes)
   - Price (e.g., $150)
   - Description (optional)
4. Click "Save"
5. Repeat for all services

### 4. Generate Your First Formula (30 seconds)

**Quick Formula (Fastest):**

1. Click "Quick Formula" on dashboard
2. Enter client details:
   - Current level (1-10)
   - Target level (1-10)
   - Hair condition
   - Desired tone
3. Click "Generate"
4. AI generates formula in 3 seconds!

**AI Assistant (Most Detailed):**

1. Click "AI Assistant"
2. Chat with AI: "I need a formula for level 5 hair to platinum"
3. AI asks clarifying questions
4. Receive personalized formula

### 5. Invite Your First Client (1 minute)

1. Go to "Clients" page
2. Click "Invite Client"
3. Enter client's email
4. Click "Send Invitation"
5. Client receives email with signup link

## For Clients

### 1. Accept Stylist Invitation

1. Check email for invitation from your stylist
2. Click "Accept Invitation" in email
3. Create account with email or Google
4. Select "I'm a Client"

### 2. Complete Your Profile

1. Add your full name
2. Upload profile photo (optional)
3. Add phone number (for SMS reminders)
4. Add hair details:
   - Hair type
   - Hair goals
   - Any allergies or sensitivities
5. Click "Save Profile"

### 3. Book Your First Appointment

1. Go to "Book Appointment"
2. Select your stylist (pre-selected from invitation)
3. Choose a service
4. Pick a date and time
5. Add any special requests (optional)
6. Click "Book Appointment"
7. Receive confirmation email immediately

### 4. Upload Reference Photos

1. Go to "My Profile"
2. Scroll to "Reference Photos"
3. Click "Upload Photo"
4. Select photos from your phone/computer
5. Add notes (optional)
6. Click "Save"

### 5. Manage Notifications

1. Go to "Settings"
2. Choose notification preferences:
   - ✅ Appointment reminders (24hrs before)
   - ✅ SMS notifications (if phone added)
   - ✅ Rebooking reminders (6 weeks after)
   - ❌ Marketing emails (optional)
3. Click "Save Preferences"

## Troubleshooting

### "I didn't receive the confirmation email"

1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Verify email address is correct
3. Try resending confirmation
4. Contact your stylist

### "I forgot my password"

1. Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enter your email
3. Check email for reset link
4. Create new password
5. Log in with new password

### "AI formula isn't generating"

1. Check all required fields are filled
2. Ensure values are realistic (levels 1-10)
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Clear browser cache
5. Try different browser

### "SMS reminders not working"

1. Verify phone number format (+1234567890)
2. Check phone number is saved in profile
3. Ensure SMS notifications enabled in settings
4. Contact your stylist if issue persists

### "Appointment not showing in calendar"

1. Check calendar sync is enabled
2. Verify Google Calendar connection
3. Manual refresh in calendar app
4. Re-connect Google Calendar
