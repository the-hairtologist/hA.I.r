# Email Automation Guide for Stylists

## Overview
The automated email system helps you stay connected with your clients by sending professional, personalized rebooking reminders 6 weeks after their last appointment.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. **Fully Automatic**
- Runs every day at 9 AM UTC
- Checks for clients who need rebooking reminders
- Sends professional, personalized emails automatically
- Tracks open rates, click rates, and conversions

### 2. **Easy Customization**
Personalize your emails to match your brand and voice:
- Custom subject lines
- Personalized headlines and messages
- Add your business logo
- Include special offers or personal notes
- Change button text
- Customize closing messages

### 3. **Client-Friendly**
- Professional, warm tone
- Easy unsubscribe options
- Direct booking link
- Mobile-optimized design
- Respects client preferences

## 📧 How to Customize Your Emails

### Accessing Email Settings
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Email Settings** (or via Email Campaigns page)
2. The default template is already professional and ready to use
3. Make changes to match your brand

### Available Customization Options

#### Subject Line
The text clients see in their inbox. Keep it friendly and engaging!
- **Default**: "✨ Time for a Touch-Up with {{stylist_name}}!"
- **Tip**: Use emojis to stand out in crowded inboxes

#### Email Headline
The main header inside the email.
- **Default**: "Hi {{client_name}}! 👋"
- **Tip**: Keep it warm and personal

#### Opening Message
The first paragraph clients read.
- **Default**: "It's been about 6 weeks since your last visit with {{stylist_name}} at {{business_name}}. Your hair is probably ready for some professional love! 💇"
- **Tip**: Mention the time frame and create a sense of care

#### Custom Message (Optional)
Add a special touch! This appears in a highlighted box:
- Special promotions ("Book this week and get 10% off!")
- Personal notes ("I loved working on your color last time!")
- Seasonal messages ("Summer is coming - let's prep your hair!")

#### Button Text
What the booking button says.
- **Default**: "📅 Book Your Appointment"
- **Tip**: Use action words and keep it short

#### Closing Message
The final message before the footer.
- **Default**: "{{stylist_name}} is looking forward to seeing you again and help you maintain that fabulous look!"
- **Tip**: Make it personal and encouraging

#### Business Logo
Upload your logo to brand your emails.
- Toggle "Show Business Logo" on/off
- Paste your logo URL
- **Tip**: Use a high-quality PNG or JPG, max 150px wide

### Using Placeholders
These special codes are automatically replaced with real information:

- **{{client_name}}** - Client's first name (e.g., "Sarah")
- **{{stylist_name}}** - Your name or your stylists name
- **{{business_name}}** - Your salon/business name

**Example**:
- You write: "Hi {{client_name}}, {{stylist_name}} can't wait to see you!"
- Client sees: "Hi Sarah, Maria can't wait to see you!"

## 👁️ Preview Your Emails
Use the **Show Preview** button to see exactly how your email will look to clients. The preview shows:
- Your customizations in action
- Sample client data
- Mobile and desktop view

## 📊 Tracking Performance

### Email Campaigns Dashboard
Access via **Dashboard** → **Email Campaigns**

**Key Metrics**:
- **Total Sent**: All rebooking emails sent
- **Open Rate**: % of clients who opened the email
- **Click Rate**: % of clients who clicked "Book"
- **Conversion Rate**: % of clients who rebooked

**Recent Campaigns Table** shows:
- When emails were sent
- Email type (6-week reminder)
- Engagement (opened, clicked, booked)

### Send Now Button
Test your emails manually:
- Click **Send Now** to trigger an immediate check
- Useful for testing your customizations
- Safe to use - only sends to clients who are due

## ⚙️ Advanced Features

### Turn Off Reminders
Toggle "Rebooking Reminders" off if you want to pause:
- Vacation time
- Fully booked periods
- Special circumstances

### Unsubscribe Management
The system automatically:
- Adds unsubscribe links to all emails
- Respects client preferences
- Tracks who has unsubscribed
- Never sends to unsubscribed clients

## 🎯 Success Tips

### High-Performing Email Elements
1. **Warm, Personal Tone** - "We missed you!" not "Schedule now"
2. **Clear Value** - Remind them why regular appointments matter
3. **Easy Action** - One clear booking button
4. **Professional Design** - Your logo + clean layout
5. **Mobile-First** - Most clients read on phones

### What NOT to Do
- ❌ Too salesy or pushy
- ❌ Multiple call-to-action buttons
- ❌ Long paragraphs
- ❌ All caps or excessive emojis
- ❌ Complicated booking process

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Emails aren't sending"
- Check that "Rebooking Reminders" is enabled
- Verify you have the RESEND_API_KEY configured
- Check Email Campaigns dashboard for errors

### "Preview not showing my changes"
- Click "Save Changes" first
- Refresh the preview
- Check for missing placeholder values

### "Clients not receiving emails"
- Verify client email addresses are correct
- Check spam/junk folders
- Ensure clients haven't unsubscribed
