## 📋 YOUR ACTION ITEMS (Complete BEFORE Launch)

### 1. Copyright Protection (CRITICAL - Do First) 🔴

**Why:** Legally proves you own your app's content and visuals  
**Cost:** $45-$65 (one-time)  
**Time:** 15-30 minutes to file, 6-8 months for registration

**Steps:**
1. Go to **https://copyright.gov/registration**
2. Register the following:
   - ✅ Your app design and UI/UX
   - ✅ Your logo and branding
   - ✅ Written content (descriptions, help text, etc.)
3. Take screenshots of your Lovable project and export files as proof of creation
4. File online application (VA - Visual Arts for design/logo, TX - Text for written content)
5. Pay $45-$65 fee per registration
6. Save confirmation email and certificate when received

**Tip:** This legally proves you created it FIRST if anyone copies you later.

---

### 2. Trademark Your Brand Name (HIGH PRIORITY) 🟠

**Why:** Protects your name "hA.I.r" so no one else can use it in the beauty space  
**Cost:** $250-$350 (one-time, per class)  
**Time:** 30 minutes to file, 8-12 months for approval

**Steps:**
1. Go to **https://uspto.gov/trademarks**
2. Search existing trademarks to ensure "hA.I.r" is available in your category
3. File trademark application for:
   - ✅ Your app name: **hA.I.r™**
   - ✅ Your logo (if possible - visual trademark protection)
4. Choose trademark class: **Class 44** (Beauty salon services)
5. Pay $250-$350 filing fee
6. Monitor status at USPTO website (takes 8-12 months)

**Tip:** Include both your app name AND logo if possible. This prevents copycats.

---

### 3. Business Setup - EIN (RECOMMENDED) 🟡

**Why:** Required for taxes, bank accounts, and Stripe business connection  
**Cost:** FREE  
**Time:** 10 minutes online

**Steps:**
1. Go to **https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online**
2. Choose business structure:
   - **Sole Proprietor** (simplest, you personally own it)
   - **LLC** (more protection, $50-$150 to set up via your state)
3. Complete online EIN application
4. Receive EIN immediately (9-digit number like: 12-3456789)
5. Save EIN letter/document - you'll need it for Stripe and taxes

**Optional LLC Setup ($50-$150):**
- If you want more legal protection, create an LLC through your state's business website
- Search: "[Your State] LLC formation"
- File Articles of Organization with your state
- Get separate business bank account

**Why EIN matters:**
- Required for Stripe business account
- Keeps business finances separate from personal
- Makes tax filing easier (Schedule C or 1099)

---

### 4. Payment Setup - Stripe Connection (CRITICAL) 🔴

**Why:** Can't accept payments without this  
**Cost:** FREE (Stripe takes 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction)  
**Time:** 20 minutes

**Your app is READY for Stripe - just needs YOUR account connection:**

**Steps:**
1. Go to **https://stripe.com**
2. Create Stripe account (or sign in)
3. Complete Stripe onboarding:
   - ✅ Business information
   - ✅ Bank account details (where money gets deposited)
   - ✅ Tax information (EIN or SSN)
4. Get your **Stripe Secret Key** and **Publishable Key**
5. Connect Stripe to Lovable:
   - In Lovable Cloud dashboard, go to **Settings → Integrations → Stripe**
   - Enter your Stripe keys
   - Test a payment to verify

**Note:** Your app code already has Stripe integration built-in. You just need to connect YOUR Stripe account.

**Tax Tracking:**
- Keep records of ALL income (Stripe provides reports)
- If online-only (no physical goods), usually won't charge sales tax initially
- Consult with tax professional for your specific situation
- Stripe handles payment processing, you handle income reporting

### 6. App Store Accounts (REQUIRED for Mobile App Stores) 🔴

**Why:** Can't publish to app stores without these  
**Cost:** $99/year (Apple), $25 one-time (Google)  
**Time:** 1-2 hours setup per platform

**Google Play Store (Android):**
1. Go to **play.google.com/console**
2. Create Google Play Developer account
3. Pay **$25 one-time registration fee**
4. Complete developer profile:
   - Business name, address, contact
   - Privacy policy URL (you have this!)
   - Terms of service URL (you have this!)
5. Prepare app listing materials:
   - Screenshots (1080x1920px, at least 2)
   - Feature graphic (1024x500px)
   - App icon (512x512px)
   - Short description (80 characters)
   - Full description (4000 characters)

**Apple App Store (iOS):**
1. Go to **developer.apple.com**
2. Enroll in Apple Developer Program
3. Pay **$99/year subscription**
4. Complete developer profile
5. Prepare app listing materials (similar to Google)
6. Important: Apple has stricter review process (7-14 days)

**When to do this:**
- If launching as native mobile app: **Do NOW**
- If launching as PWA (web app) only: **Wait until needed**
- Your app already works as PWA (installable from browser)

### 7. Refund Policy (RECOMMENDED) 🟡

**Why:** Builds trust, required for some app stores  
**Cost:** FREE  
**Time:** 15 minutes

**Current Status:**
- Your Terms of Service mentions refund policy (stylist discretion)
- Consider adding dedicated `/refund-policy` page

**Create via:**
- **https://www.termsfeed.com/refund-policy-generator** (free)
- **https://www.websitepolicies.com/refund-policy-generator** (free)

**Key points to include:**
- Subscription refunds (within 14 days of purchase)
- Service refunds (at stylist's discretion)
- How to request refund
- Processing time (7-14 business days)
- Non-refundable items (no-show appointments)

**Steps:**
1. Generate refund policy using generator above
2. Review and customize for your business
3. Create `/refund-policy` page in Lovable
4. Link from footer and Terms page
