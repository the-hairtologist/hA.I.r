## Ad Image Guidelines

### What Works Best:

- Before/after transformations
- Happy clients (with permission)
- Stylist at work shots
- Clean, bright salon spaces
- Close-ups of beautiful hair

### Avoid:

- Cluttered backgrounds
- Poor lighting
- Text-heavy images
- Stock photos that look generic

## Platform Specifications

### Instagram Feed Posts

- Square: 1080 x 1080px
- Text: 125 characters for main copy
- Hashtags: 5-10 relevant ones (#haircolor #salonlife #hairgoals)

### Facebook Ads

- Image: 1200 x 628px (recommended)
- Headline: 40 characters
- Description: 125 characters
- Link description: 30 characters

### Instagram Stories/TikTok

- Vertical: 1080 x 1920px
- Keep text large and readable
- Hook in first 3 seconds

### Google Display Ads

- 300 x 250px (Medium Rectangle)
- 728 x 90px (Leaderboard)
- 160 x 600px (Wide Skyscraper)

## Success Metrics to Track

- Click-through rate (CTR) - Aim for 1-2%
- Cost per click (CPC) - Industry average $1-3
- Conversion rate - Booking to visit ratio
- Return on ad spend (ROAS) - Aim for 3:1 minimum

## Pro Tips

1. **Test Multiple Versions** - Try 3-4 different headlines and see what works
2. **Use Real Results** - Before/afters perform 3x better than stock photos
3. **Mobile First** - 80% of bookings happen on phones
4. **Local Targeting** - Focus on 5-15 mile radius around salon
5. **Retargeting** - Show ads to people who visited your booking page
